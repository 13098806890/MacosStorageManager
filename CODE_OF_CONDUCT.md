# Code of Conduct

CleanUpForMacOS welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Share relevant context, and be open to revising a proposal when new evidence changes the picture.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using GitHub's reporting tools. For a security vulnerability, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports and may remove content, restrict participation, or close discussions when needed to protect contributors.
