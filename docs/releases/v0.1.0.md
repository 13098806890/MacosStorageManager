# CleanUpForMacOS 0.1.0

First public release of CleanUpForMacOS.

## Highlights

- Scan configured cache directories on demand, review descriptions, estimated sizes, and cleanup impact, then confirm before moving ordinary cache targets to macOS Trash.
- Import and export JSON profiles, enable individual rules, and manage multiple profiles.
- Expand visible child folders and compare their sizes; large trees load in pages.
- Review Xcode Simulator devices and their data sizes. Confirmed device deletion is permanent. Simulator runtimes remain managed by Xcode.
- View disk capacity and local available-space history recorded after manual scans.
- Includes developer cache rules for Cargo registry sources and Gradle Wrapper distributions. Removing them may require re-extraction or downloads; offline builds may be affected.

## Safety and limitations

- Scans are started by the user; no background or scheduled cleanup runs.
- Scan sizes are estimates, not file inventories; hidden files may be omitted.
- Ordinary cache targets are moved to Trash after explicit confirmation. Simulator device removal is permanent.
- Cleanup logs, imported profiles, and disk history stay local. Cleanup logs contain expanded local paths and must not be shared publicly.
- The app disables App Sandbox to access its approved home-relative cache paths. It is intended for direct distribution and is not compatible with the Mac App Store.
- The repository does not yet include a `LICENSE`; redistribution and reuse terms have not been established.
